# Images for Récolte (Site 1)

Generate these with any AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney…), then:

1. Convert to **WebP** and compress to **under 200 KB** each (squoosh.app or tinypng.com).
2. Save them with the **exact file name** below in `public/images/`.
3. Commit and push. The site picks them up automatically. Until then, each slot shows a soft beige block.

**Style to keep for every image:** minimal editorial product photography, soft natural daylight from a window, warm muted autumn palette (cream, terracotta, sand, warm brown), lots of negative space, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no people's faces.
